### Critical Discovery Insight

**Brave Search is the production-grade search engine for this MVP.** Unlike DuckDuckGo (CAPTCHA after 3-4 queries) and Google/Bing (block all automated access), Brave Search returns detailed, structured results with URLs, snippets, and company names. It handles site: filters and OR operators. It showed TTF, TTJ Buyers Guide, Fordaq, and individual company website results all in one query. This is the primary search provider for the MVP's Company Discovery Service.
