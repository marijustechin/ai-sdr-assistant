- **Thermo-wood manufacturing found.** ESSPO produces thermally modified wood — the exact process our A+B grade is suited for.
- **Timber import/export companies exist.** Diverus and Vilterra are established timber traders who likely buy African hardwood.
- **No website scraping yield from Rekvizitai.lt listings.** Company pages show categories, addresses, and descriptions but rarely list websites in the category view. Individual company pages would need to be fetched separately for website URLs and contact details.

## Knowledge Base Updates Required

- **Rekvizitai.lt is a validated discovery source.** Category pages are accessible via GET (no form submission needed). Pagination works (278 pages for wood category). Company data includes: name, address, categories, description, contact info links. Individual company pages (URLs like /en/company/diverus/) load with full details.
- **Lithuania has a sauna manufacturing cluster.** 3 sauna/tub manufacturers found in first 40 results. Abachi's sauna application has domestic Lithuanian buyers.
- **Thermo-wood is produced domestically.** ESSPO is a Lithuanian thermo-wood manufacturer. This is a direct product application match.
- **Lithuanian timber companies are smaller than German ones.** Most are MB (small partnership) or small UAB, not the national-scale wholesalers found in Germany. One container (~45 m³) is a significant order for most Lithuanian timber companies.
- **Two distinct Lithuanian buyer types:** (a) timber importers/exporters who resell internationally (Diverus, Vilterra), (b) sauna/tub/thermo manufacturers who consume wood as feedstock (ESSPO, EDJUSTA). Both are valid but need different sales approaches.
