## Lessons Learned

1. **DuckDuckGo HTML endpoint works but is fragile.** Rate limits after 3-4 queries before CAPTCHA. Not suitable for production but proves search-based discovery is viable. Production would need Serper, Brave Search API, or Google CSE.

2. **Germany has the densest market.** 11 of 12 companies found are German. Abachi is primarily a German-trade-name phenomenon (other countries use Obeche/Ayous). German is the right first market.

3. **Many hits are timber merchants, not end-manufacturers.** The search found companies that already TRADE Abachi, not companies that USE it to make products (mouldings, furniture). The buyer persona needs refinement — "timber merchant who sells tropical hardwood" vs "manufacturer who buys tropical hardwood as raw material." Both are valid buyers but require different approaches.

4. **Cross Trade GmbH is the standout lead.** They explicitly import Ayous from Africa. They're in Bremen (port city). They're in the right business. A phone call to them would validate the entire Phase 0 hypothesis.

5. **Named procurement contacts remain elusive.** None of the 12 companies listed a timber buyer or purchasing manager by name. Generic info@ addresses and contact forms dominate. This confirms: named contacts should be treated as a bonus, not a requirement.

6. **Multi-country search strategy needs parallel implementation.** By the time we got 12 German companies, DDG was rate-limiting multi-country queries. Production would need to parallelize searches across providers and countries simultaneously.

## Knowledge Base Updates Required

Based on this batch, the following should be added to knowledge-base.md:

- **DuckDuckGo HTML endpoint**: 3-4 queries per session before CAPTCHA. Use as validation only, not production.
- **German Abachi market**: Highly developed. Many merchants already stock it. Sales approach needs competitive pricing/availability angle.
- **Two distinct buyer types**: (a) traders who import/resell tropical hardwood, (b) manufacturers who consume it. Different outreach strategies needed.
- **Email pattern**: info@company.tld dominates German timber trader websites. verkauf@ occasionally. Named individuals: very rare.
