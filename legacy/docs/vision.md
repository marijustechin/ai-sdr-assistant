# AI SDR Assistant

### Goal

Reduce the time spent on repetitive sales activities and deliver qualified business opportunities to the salesperson.

The system does not replace the salesperson. It prepares everything so the salesperson can focus on negotiations and closing deals.

## 1. Opportunity Definition

The salesperson provides information such as:

- Product (wood, nuts, textiles, etc.)
- Quantity available
- Target countries
- Target industries
- Special requirements

Example:

"I have one container of African walnut available in Germany."

## 2. Market Research

The system identifies:

- industries that use the product
- companies likely to buy it
- geographical markets
- import/export trends (when available)
- competitors

## 3. Lead Generation

The system builds a list of potential buyers.

For each company it collects:

- company name
- website
- location
- size
- business activity
- decision makers (when publicly available)
- email addresses
- LinkedIn/company profiles
- phone numbers (when available)

## 4. Lead Qualification & Scoring

Not every company is worth contacting.

The system estimates how promising each lead is.

Possible criteria:

- imports similar products
- company size
- purchasing power
- location
- product fit
- previous importing activity
- strategic relevance

Each lead receives a score.

Example:

- 95/100 — Excellent
- 80/100 — Good
- 40/100 — Low priority

## 5. AI Company Research

Before any email is written, AI creates a short company profile.

For example:

- what the company does
- why they might need this product
- recent news
- possible buying signals
- possible objections

## 6. Personalized Outreach

Instead of generic emails, AI prepares personalized drafts.

Example:

"I noticed your company recently expanded its furniture production..."

instead of

"Dear Sir/Madam..."

The salesperson can approve or edit the message before sending.

## 7. Communication Tracking

The system remembers:

- who was contacted
- when
- email status
- replies
- follow-up dates
- notes

No spreadsheets required.

## 8. AI Reply Analysis

When replies arrive, AI summarizes them.

Example:

Customer wrote a long email.

AI produces:

- interested ✔
- asks for price
- asks about shipping
- requests certificates
- recommends follow-up this week

The salesperson reads the summary first, then the original email if needed.

## 9. Sales Dashboard

A simple overview:

- New leads
- Qualified leads
- Emails sent
- Waiting for reply
- Interested companies
- Follow-ups due today

## 10. Human Salesperson Takes Over

Once a company shows genuine interest:

- negotiation
- pricing
- contracts
- logistics
- relationship building

remain completely human.
