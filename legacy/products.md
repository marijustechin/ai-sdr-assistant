# Abachi Wood Products – Product and Sales Information

## 1. Kiln-Dried Rough-Sawn Abachi Lumber

Kiln-dried, rough-sawn Abachi lumber with a moisture content of 10–12%.

### A Grade

* Predominantly white appearance
* Minimal natural discoloration
* Thickness: 25 mm
* Width: 210 mm
* Lengths: 2–6 metres

### A+B Grade

* Natural discoloration is permitted
* Inactive (dead) wormholes are permitted on one face
* Suitable for thermal modification
* Thickness: 25 mm
* Width: 95 mm
* Lengths: from 2.1 to 6 metres

## 2. Thermally Modified Abachi Panelling – STS 3D Profile

Thermally modified Abachi wood panelling with an STS 3D profile.

* Wood species: Abachi
* Treatment: thermal modification
* Profile: STS 3D
* Thickness: 20 mm
* Covering width: 80 mm

## Stock Location and Delivery

The products are currently located in Vilnius, Lithuania. Depending on the order, delivery can be arranged to ports throughout Europe.

## Available Quantity

* Approximately 45 m³
* Equivalent to approximately one shipping container

## Target Markets

Potential markets include:

* Scandinavian countries
* Germany
* The United Kingdom
* Other European countries

## Origin

The timber originates from the Republic of Congo.

## Certification

The products are not FSC-certified. Import requirements and required documentation (including EUTR due diligence) must be verified separately for each destination country.
